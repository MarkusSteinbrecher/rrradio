Wake up to radio

Set rrradio to start playing a station at a time you choose — a gentle way to wake up to music or the morning news instead of a buzzer.

Set a wake alarm

  1. In rrradio, open the station (or favorites list) you want to wake up to.
  2. Open the Wake alarm.
  3. Pick the time you want it to start.
  4. Choose the station to play.
  5. For the best chance it plays on its own, turn on “Keep playing until wake.”
  6. Save the alarm. You can save more than one and switch them on or off from your Wake Alarms list.

Getting the most reliable wake

iPhone is careful about letting apps run overnight, so a radio alarm can’t be guaranteed the way the built-in Clock alarm is. A few things make it as reliable as possible:

  • Keep “Keep playing until wake” turned on. This keeps rrradio gently ready through the night so it can start your station at the right moment. (It uses a little extra battery, so keep your phone on the charger.)
  • Don’t swipe rrradio closed before bed. If the app is fully closed, it can’t start on its own.
  • Leave the backup notification on. If your iPhone has paused the app, you’ll still get a notification at the wake time so you can tap to start playing.
  • Try it once first. Set an alarm a couple of minutes ahead, lock your phone, and check it works the way you expect before relying on it for the morning.

Good to know

  • Keep your phone charged overnight — staying ready to play uses some battery.
  • The first time, your iPhone may ask permission to send notifications. Allow it so the backup reminder works.
  • You can keep several wake alarms saved and switch between them.

That’s it — pick a station, pick a time, and wake up to radio.
