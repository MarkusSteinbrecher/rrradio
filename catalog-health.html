        How the score works

        Every published station is checked automatically: curated stations daily, the long tail weekly, and any failing stream daily until it recovers. Each of the four checks gives a station good (1 point), weak (½) or bad (0). A check that doesn’t apply, or hasn’t run yet, is left out rather than counted against the station.

        The catalog score is the weighted average: stream 40%, now playing 25%, logo 20%, homepage 15%. Streams that fail three days in a row leave the catalog on their own and come back when they play again; hard failures (404, DNS) retire a long-tail station after 3 days, soft ones (timeouts, 5xx) after 5 days and a second opinion.

        The data lives on the health-data branch and the method is documented in docs/station-health.md. A station plays fine but shows as failing? Open an issue.
