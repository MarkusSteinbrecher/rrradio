rrradio.org — minimal, ad-free internet radio in your browser

Listen to live radio streams from around the world. Curated stations include FM4, Ö1, Bayern 1, BBC Radio 1–6, BBC World Service, Radio France, RAI, Grrif and many more — alongside the long tail of community-maintained stations from Radio Browser. No signup, no app install, no tracking.

      Version dev
      
            
      
            
          
      

          
      In a nutshell: internet radio app, no ads and no tracking. The stations are managed on rrradio.org, a non-profit open source project.
      

          
            
            Visit rrradio.org
            
          

          
          
            
            iPhone app & new look
            
          

          
      
            
      Features
      
            
      
              
              
      
                
      Browse & search
      
                
      Search a global catalog of stations and filter by country, genre and stream quality.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
              
      
                
      Favorites & library
      
                
      Save favorites and build lists for quick access and skipping between stations.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
              
      
                
      Sleep timer
      
                
      Auto-stop playback after the time you set.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
              
      
                
      Now playing & lyrics
      
                
      Current artist, title, cover art, lyrics, the on-air show for supported broadcasters, and Apple Music / Spotify / YouTube Music links when metadata is available.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
              
      
                
      Custom streams
      
                
      Paste your own HTTPS stream URL; your private list stays on your device. If you have a station we missed, we would love to hear from you!
